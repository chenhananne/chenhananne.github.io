Han Chen

PhD Researcher in Economics · University of Strathclyde

My research is in environmental economics and international trade. I use quantitative trade models to study how climate policies reshape emissions, production, and trade across countries and supply chains.

Research

Climate coalitions and carbon leakage

How does the choice of new climate coalition members affect carbon leakage, global emissions, and the incentives to join? This project examines how border carbon adjustments shape those outcomes.

Carbon border adjustment and manufacturing supply chains

How do carbon costs pass from upstream producers to downstream manufacturers? This project examines how the EU’s Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism and a potential downstream extension affect manufacturing competitiveness and emissions.
